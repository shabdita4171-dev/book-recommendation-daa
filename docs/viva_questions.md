# Viva Questions
1. Why a graph? Books are vertices and similarity relationships are edges.
2. Why BFS? It explores level by level.
3. Why DFS? It demonstrates deep traversal.
4. Why Dijkstra? It handles non-negative weighted paths.
5. Why not BFS for weighted paths? BFS ignores weights.
6. How is similarity calculated? Genre, author, rating and tags.
7. What is V? Number of books.
8. What is E? Number of similarity edges.
9. BFS complexity? O(V+E).
10. DFS complexity? O(V+E).
11. Dijkstra complexity? O((V+E) log V).
12. Merge Sort complexity? O(n log n).
13. Is ML used? No.
14. What is an adjacency list? A structure storing each vertex's neighbors.
15. Why use a priority queue? Efficient Top-K/shortest-path selection.
